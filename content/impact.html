How I lead engineering

Across ten years at AIG, progressing from Lead Frontend Architect to Global Director, and earlier leadership roles at Bank of America and Sonepar, my work has centered on helping engineering teams deliver reliable software at enterprise scale.

Leading globally distributed teams

As Global Director of Frontend Engineering at AIG, I coordinated globally distributed engineering teams, aligning technical priorities across regions and keeping teams focused on shared enterprise goals. Developing people has been a constant throughout my career: as Director of Frontend Technologies, I coached and developed engineers and helped them solve significant engineering challenges across global teams.

Engineering strategy and enterprise standards

I have repeatedly established the standards that let many teams work consistently: standards for testing, performance, accessibility, and code quality as Lead Frontend Architect at AIG; development, testing, and quality standards at Bank of America; cross-functional engineering standards as Director of Frontend Technologies; and, most recently, Global Standards for AI-Assisted Software Engineering.

Shared engineering capabilities

I built and scaled a reusable UI component ecosystem adopted by more than 80 teams worldwide. Shared capabilities like this reduce duplicated effort, make good practices the default, and let independent teams deliver more consistently: the core goals of platform engineering and developer experience.

Complex software delivery

I architected and led development of 7+ enterprise applications at AIG. Earlier in my AIG tenure, I architected enterprise applications and then transferred implementation responsibility to development teams. That pattern builds lasting team ownership rather than dependency on a single architect.

Product and business alignment

Engineering decisions succeed when they reflect business and customer needs. At AIG I aligned business, UX, UI, and engineering stakeholders, partnered with architecture, business, design, and delivery teams, and aligned technical initiatives with enterprise priorities and customer needs. At Sonepar / Industrial Distribution Group, I led engineering strategy for a significant e-commerce modernization whose digital improvements contributed to tripling annual revenue.
