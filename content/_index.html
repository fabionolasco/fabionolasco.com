Engineering Excellence at AI Speed

AI is transforming software development, but generating code faster is only part of the opportunity.

The greater challenge is redesigning how engineering organizations operate—how requirements become specifications, how architectural decisions are made, how AI agents execute work, and how teams verify quality at a pace traditional development processes were never designed to support.

My work focuses on bringing these disciplines together: advancing engineering productivity through agentic AI while preserving the principles that make software reliable, secure, scalable, and maintainable.

Since 2019, I have taught Computer Science at UNC Charlotte alongside my industry work. As AI-Assisted Engineering Curriculum Director, I created AI-Assisted Software Engineering coursework for undergraduate and graduate students that grew into a Computer Science concentration.

Teaching and enterprise engineering inform each other: the classroom forces clarity about principles, and enterprise work keeps the curriculum grounded in the realities of requirements, architecture, testing, evaluation, and production readiness.
