Hi, I'm Fabio

I'm an engineering leader, software architect, author, and Associate Professor with more than two decades of experience developing software and helping engineering teams succeed.

My career has evolved from hands-on engineering and architecture to leading globally distributed teams, establishing enterprise-wide engineering standards, and guiding organizations through technical change.

At AIG, I led the architecture and development of enterprise applications, helped scale shared engineering capabilities across more than 80 teams, and created global standards for AI-assisted software engineering.

Alongside my industry work, I teach Computer Science at UNC Charlotte, where I developed AI-Assisted Software Engineering coursework that grew into a full academic concentration.

I believe engineering excellence comes from combining technical rigor with a strong understanding of people, product needs, and organizational realities.

I've been building software since my first professional project in 1996, and I still enjoy it.
